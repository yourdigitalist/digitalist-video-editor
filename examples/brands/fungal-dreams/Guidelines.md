# Guidelines

- Keep navy `#1F1D2B` as the default motion background unless a campaign plate needs a color blob field.
- One accent focus at a time (`#FF7551`). Support purple for secondary UI only.
- Icons: navy stroke + mint fill, flat, rounded corners.
- Botanical mushroom plates sit on solid liquid blobs (mint, pink, orange, yellow) — never busy photography behind type.
- Product cutouts (grow bags, lab tools) animate with pop / drawn stroke; do not redraw the FD logo mark.
